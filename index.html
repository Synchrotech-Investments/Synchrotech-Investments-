About Synchrotech

A Zimbabwean company built around adding value and connecting partners.

Synchrotech Investments focuses on product beneficiation: moving goods up the value chain so more of their worth is realised, and shared, in Zimbabwe and the region.

We pair this with strategic partnerships and supply-chain consulting, linking producers, buyers and investors across our home market and international trade partners.
